# BrightSmile Dental Clinic

BrightSmile Dental Clinic is at 12, 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038, next to the Indiranagar metro station exit B. Phone: +91 80 4000 1234. Paid parking is available in the basement.

# Opening hours

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Closed on Sundays and national holidays. Last appointment slot starts at 6:30 PM. Each standard appointment slot is 30 minutes.

# Doctors

Dr. Kavya Rao, BDS MDS, is the lead dentist and specialises in root canal treatment and cosmetic dentistry. She works Monday to Friday.
Dr. Arjun Menon, BDS MDS, is an orthodontist (braces and aligners). He works Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
Dr. Sneha Iyer, BDS, handles general check-ups, cleaning and children's dentistry. She works Monday to Saturday.

# Services and prices

Consultation and check-up: 500 rupees. The consultation fee is waived if treatment is started the same day.
Teeth cleaning and polishing (scaling): 1,500 rupees.
Tooth-coloured filling: 1,200 to 2,000 rupees per tooth.
Root canal treatment: 6,000 to 9,000 rupees per tooth, depending on the tooth. A crown is extra.
Ceramic crown: 8,000 rupees. Zirconia crown: 14,000 rupees.
Tooth extraction: 1,000 rupees for a simple extraction, 4,000 to 6,000 rupees for a wisdom tooth.
Teeth whitening (in-clinic): 10,000 rupees.
Metal braces: starting at 35,000 rupees. Clear aligners: starting at 90,000 rupees.
Digital X-ray: 300 rupees per image.

# Payments and insurance

We accept cash, UPI, all major debit and credit cards. No-cost EMI is available on treatments above 15,000 rupees through partner banks.
We are a cashless partner for Star Health and HDFC Ergo dental add-on plans. For other insurers we give a detailed bill so you can claim reimbursement.

# Appointment policy

Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit and bring any previous X-rays.
Cancellations or rescheduling are free up to 4 hours before the appointment. Late cancellations or no-shows may be charged 200 rupees.
Walk-ins are accepted, but booked patients are seen first.

# Emergencies

For severe tooth pain we keep two emergency slots open every day; call the clinic and we will try to see you the same day.
For heavy bleeding that will not stop, facial swelling with fever, or injury to the jaw, go to the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.

# Children

We see children from age 3. Dr. Sneha Iyer handles paediatric visits. A parent or guardian must stay with children under 16.

# After-care tips

After a filling, avoid eating for one hour. After an extraction, do not rinse, spit or use a straw for 24 hours, and avoid hot food that day. After whitening, avoid tea, coffee and coloured foods for 48 hours.
